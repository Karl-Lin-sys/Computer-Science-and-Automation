# Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning

## Overview
This module explores advanced AI and ML concepts, grounding algorithmic choice and teaching methods exclusively in RCTs (≤ 20 years) and Systematic Reviews/Meta-analyses (≤ 3 years).

## Evidence-Based Curriculum Components

### 1. Model Selection and Evaluation
**Evidence Base:** Recent Systematic Reviews (2024) on benchmarking frameworks.
**Content:** 
- Avoiding "Fundamental Attribution Error" in model evaluation: When a model fails in deployment, students are taught to analyze systemic data biases and distribution shifts rather than blaming "edge-case users".
- RCTs demonstrating the efficacy of active learning and human-in-the-loop interventions in mitigating dataset bias.

### 2. Deep Learning Architectures
**Evidence Base:** Meta-analyses (2023) comparing Transformer vs. CNN efficiency in varied domains.
**Content:**
- Analyzing state-of-the-art architectures with a focus on reproducibility.
- Structural analysis of failure modes in deep neural networks.

## Assessment
Students will design a machine learning pipeline where the validation methodology must be justified by at least one meta-analysis from the past three years.
