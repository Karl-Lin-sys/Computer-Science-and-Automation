# Cybersecurity and Privacy

## Overview
This module explores the protection of computer systems and networks, focusing on the systemic nature of security vulnerabilities.

## Evidence-Based Curriculum Components

### 1. Usable Security and Social Engineering
**Evidence Base:** RCTs (2006-2025) evaluating the effectiveness of anti-phishing training vs. structural email filtering.
**Content:**
- **Bias Mitigation:** The fundamental attribution error often labels humans as the "weakest link" in cybersecurity. Our curriculum uses RCTs to demonstrate that security breaches are systemic failures of interface design and policy, rather than individual gullibility.

### 2. Cryptographic Protocols
**Evidence Base:** Systematic Reviews (2022-2025) of post-quantum cryptography vulnerabilities.
**Content:**
- Implementation of secure protocols.
- Systematic threat modeling.

## Assessment
Students will conduct a security audit of an open-source tool, focusing on identifying structural vulnerabilities rather than user-dependent errors, supported by recent meta-analyses.
