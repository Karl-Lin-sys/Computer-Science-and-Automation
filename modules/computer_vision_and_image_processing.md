# Computer Vision and Image Processing

## Overview
This module addresses the extraction of high-level understanding from digital images and videos, strictly backed by high-level empirical evidence.

## Evidence-Based Curriculum Components

### 1. Algorithmic Fairness in Facial Recognition
**Evidence Base:** Meta-analyses (2023) detailing demographic disparities in computer vision models.
**Content:**
- **Bias Mitigation:** Avoiding fundamental attribution error by recognizing that misclassification of minority groups is a systemic dataset and algorithmic failure, not a user or environmental error.

### 2. Medical Image Processing
**Evidence Base:** RCTs (2010-2025) validating AI-assisted diagnostic tools vs. human-only diagnostics.
**Content:**
- Efficacy of segmentation algorithms.
- Statistical significance in diagnostic improvement.

## Assessment
Students must implement a vision pipeline and provide a critical analysis of its demographic fairness, citing at least two systematic reviews from the last three years.
