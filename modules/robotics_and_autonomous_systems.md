# Robotics and Autonomous Systems

## Overview
This module covers the design, control, and implementation of autonomous physical systems. The pedagogy emphasizes mitigating fundamental attribution error during human-robot interaction (HRI).

## Evidence-Based Curriculum Components

### 1. Human-Robot Interaction (HRI)
**Evidence Base:** RCTs (2005-2025) studying human trust in automated systems.
**Content:** 
- **Bias Mitigation:** When a human operator misuses a robot, the fundamental attribution error assumes operator incompetence. Our curriculum uses RCT evidence to demonstrate how interface design, latency, and feedback loops are the true systemic causes of HRI failures.

### 2. Autonomous Navigation and Path Planning
**Evidence Base:** Systematic Reviews (2022-2025) of SLAM algorithms and reinforcement learning in continuous spaces.
**Content:**
- Algorithmic safety bounds.
- Systemic failure analysis in unpredictable environments.

## Assessment
Development of an autonomous navigation simulation, evaluated against safety benchmarks established in recent meta-analyses.
