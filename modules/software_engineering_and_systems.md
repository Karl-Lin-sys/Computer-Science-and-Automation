# Software Engineering and Systems

## Overview
This module covers the lifecycle of software systems, from architecture to deployment, framed through the lens of human factors and systemic reliability.

## Evidence-Based Curriculum Components

### 1. Agile and DevOps Methodologies
**Evidence Base:** Systematic Reviews (2023) of productivity and code quality metrics in CI/CD pipelines.
**Content:**
- Evidence-based software lifecycle models.
- **Bias Mitigation:** In blameless post-mortems, students learn to counteract the fundamental attribution error. If a developer breaks the build, the failure is attributed to the lack of automated testing or structural safeguards, not the developer's skill.

### 2. Software Testing and Quality Assurance
**Evidence Base:** RCTs (2005-2025) comparing Test-Driven Development (TDD) against traditional testing methodologies.
**Content:**
- Empirical validation of testing frameworks.
- Defect density analysis.

## Assessment
A group project to refactor a legacy system, employing blameless post-mortems and TDD, justified by RCT evidence.
